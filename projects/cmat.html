02 · Cell manufacturing · Georgia Tech · May–July 2025

CMaT

A quantitative image-analysis pipeline for measuring mitochondrial uptake in 3D tissue.

At the Center for Cell Manufacturing Technologies at Georgia Institute of Technology, I developed a quantitative approach to measure mitochondrial uptake in a complex 3D tissue setting. The challenge was not just delivering mitochondria to the defect site, but distinguishing internalized mitochondria from particles that remained extracellular or nearby.

I created the analysis workflow to define a biologically meaningful spatial threshold, visualize uptake, and compare tissue near and farther from the injury site. The result was a reproducible framework for assessing localized delivery and uptake in a calvarial defect model.

This project strengthened my interest in translational cell manufacturing and in building analysis tools that make biological interpretation more consistent, defensible, and useful for future therapeutic development.
