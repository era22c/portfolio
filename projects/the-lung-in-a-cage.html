03 · Clinical opportunity assessment

The Lung in a Cage

A needs-driven assessment of device and biologic routes for organized pleural disease.

I examined a clinical problem at the boundary between bench science and product strategy: benign pleural disease can progress to a rigid fibrous rind that mechanically traps the lung, leaving patients with limited options when standard therapy fails.

For this solo BME 501 project, I conducted more than 70 hours of clinical observations over the summer and used those observations to frame the workflow, stakeholder constraints, and unmet need. I evaluated both a device route and a biologic route, comparing how each could address the mechanical and biological barriers behind trapped lung.

The result was a translational opportunity assessment that connected clinical need finding with intervention trade-offs, market context, and a value proposition. It demonstrates how I approach ambiguous problems before committing to a product direction.
