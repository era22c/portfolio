IlleoBond

A mucoadhesive patch concept for localized drug delivery in ileal Crohn’s disease.

I contributed to the development of a mucoadhesive drug-delivery concept for localized treatment of ileal Crohn’s disease, where current approaches often rely on systemic exposure or invasive intervention. The team’s design challenge was to create a patch that could anchor to ileal tissue while supporting localized therapeutic delivery.

With one other team member, I co-developed the prototype fabrication protocol, including core pressing, mucoadhesive preparation, and backing-layer fabrication. I also ran cell culture and image analysis, planned and ran diffusion studies, and participated in in vitro testing alongside my teammates.

I led most of the weekly meetings with the physicians involved in the project. That clinical coordination kept the design grounded in real workflows and helped connect materials, testing, and prototype decisions to the needs of the people who would use the system.
